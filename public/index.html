03 · Method and guardrails

Designed to test—not assume—the value of AI

More features or a more complex algorithm do not guarantee a better forecast. The comparison isolates where any improvement comes from.

01

Time-aware testing

Expanding-window backtests preserve chronology. Each training label is included only after its three-month outcome has matured.

02

Fair baselines

No-change and recent-momentum baselines sit beside linear statistical models and a small random-forest ML model.

03

Cross-domain test

Compare target-history-only models with versions that add lagged policy and sector/economic indicators.

04

Bounded interpretation

Associations are not causal proof. Trade values do not measure welfare, industrial capability, or strategic autonomy on their own.
